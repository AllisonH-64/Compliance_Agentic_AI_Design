Compliance Agentic AI Design Concept Summary

The design concept for the Compliance Agentic AI is a practical human-in-the-loop compliance system that automates repetitive checks while keeping final accountability with compliance staff.

1. Core Purpose

It continuously evaluates business cases against policy rules, determines likely compliance status, explains why, and decides whether to auto-close or escalate for human review.

2. Main Idea

The system is agentic, meaning specialized agents handle different stages of one compliance case:

  1. Intake of a new event or request
  2. Policy interpretation and rule selection
  3. Evidence collection and normalization
  4. Rule evaluation and reasoning
  5. Risk scoring and routing
  6. Human review support
  7. Governance and learning feedback

3. Decision Philosophy

It is evidence-first and audit-by-default:

  1. Every decision is tied to policy obligations and supporting evidence
  2. Outputs include status, confidence, severity, and next action
  3. High-risk or ambiguous cases are routed to human analysts
  4. All actions are logged for defensibility and traceability

4. Current MVP Implementation in This Repository

The implemented slice focuses on employee conduct compliance with deterministic controls:

  1. Harassment and bullying incident evaluation
  2. Discrimination allegation handling with heightened escalation sensitivity
  3. Client-treatment and international-governance conduct controls
  4. API endpoints support evaluation, review queue, assignment, start, submission, and reopen flows
  5. Role-based access and SQLite audit history are included

5. Intended Next Design Direction

The next design direction deepens the conduct program with stronger signal-level explainability, investigation event granularity, and richer governance analytics across risk bands, reopen reasons, and reviewer outcomes.

In short: this concept is not AI replacing compliance, but AI handling scale and consistency, while humans retain judgment and decision accountability.
